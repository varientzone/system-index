Reserved free-space rule: internal SSD should never fall below approximately 150 GB free. These are organizational caps, not physical partitions.
DJ software plan: Serato DJ is the traditional, mainstream DJ setup: mix both local files and tracks streamed from organized TIDAL playlists, keeping the local music footprint smaller. Syndicate is a separate creative playground for local music, live arrangements, sequencing, effects, and especially experimenting with VST3 synthesizers while mixing. Wait for a stable, polished release before adopting it. Its current early-access license is $50 one-time; native TIDAL integration is not currently documented, though future support is possible.
Image generation does not live on the Vivobook. All local image-generation workloads are assigned to Predator-16.
