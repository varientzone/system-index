Storage locations

Internal SSD: Games, GGUF models, REAPER libraries, VMware, Windows and apps.

SanDisk Ultra Fit 1 TB: Music and emulation ROMs. This drive is excluded from the internal SSD calculation.

How to use

1. In Windows, open This PC → Local Disk (C:) → Properties and note the capacity and used space. 2. Check Properties for your Games, GGUF, REAPER libraries and VMware folders, adding together any folders belonging to the same category. 3. Enter the four category totals and your actual C: used space. 4. The dashboard automatically calculates Other = actual used − those four categories, and Free = capacity − actual used.

Important: This dashboard does not scan your SSD. Enter measurements in GB using consistent units. The dashboard does not scan your drive. Values are saved in this browser when storage access is available. Use Export JSON to back up or transfer your entries to another browser or device.

All measurements begin blank. Automatic calculation requires JavaScript.

Storage limits are organizational budgets, not disk partitions. Capacity and usage estimates may differ from values shown by Windows.
